Oar settings and calibration

Alignment and stiffness calibration do different jobs. Keep both with the oar and sensor setup you are using.

Align Sensors

Alignment accounts for how the two sensors sit on the shaft. The guided motions establish a common reference and measure the sensors’ resting offsets.

In the iOS app, select the correct oar in Oar Settings, open Align Sensors, and follow its recording prompts. Keep both sensors mounted securely throughout.

iOS and Android: motion alignment →

Calibrate Oar Stiffness

Stiffness calibration links measured bend to moment and work. Use the calibration saved with your oar, enter its stiffness and dimensions, or make a controlled known-weight calibration.

An active Moment/Bend Factor override takes priority over the saved calibration for the selected oar. Review the active value and its source in Active Oar Details.

Before recording

  • Select the active oar and confirm Port/Starboard and Scull/Sweep.
  • Check that both sensors connect to that oar and that their straps are secure.
  • Review the alignment/calibration date and active Moment/Bend Factor in Active Oar Details.
  • Repeat alignment after moving or rotating a sensor; do not reuse an old alignment just because a curve looks reasonable.

iOS workout files are saved with alignment already applied. Cloud Analysis does not reapply it.
