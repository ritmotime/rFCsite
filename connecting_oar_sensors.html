Connecting Oar Sensors

Use this page for the app-side process of finding and connecting the Bluetooth oar sensors before launching.

Basic connection flow

  1. Turn on both oar sensors before placing the oars in the oarlocks or pushing off from the dock.
  2. Open the app and go to Main Menu → Connect Oar Sensors.
  3. Tap Turn on scanning to search for nearby devices.
  4. If pairing for the first time, disable Scan only for saved devices.
  5. Select both sensors when they appear. They may appear by MAC address.
  6. Tap DONE.
  7. After the sensors are connected, go to Oar Sensor Setup to confirm side, orientation, and alignment.

After connecting

Choose port or starboard, then check that both sensors are reasonably aligned before launch. The app can auto-calibrate while rowing, but the sensors should start close enough that the readings are stable.
