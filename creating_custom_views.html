Build and activate a view

  1. Open Session Views → Create & Edit Views.
  2. Choose a template such as Live Technique, Drive Focus or a metric grid.
  3. Name the view, tap its metric slots and choose the values to show.
  4. For a plot view, choose the live curves and check their units.
  5. Save it, then select it under Active Workout Views. Arrange the views in the order you want during the session.

Older layouts may retain names such as LMPlot or BPlot. Available data still depends on the connected sensors and enabled recording options.

Add rate, piece time, distance or GPS pace as supporting numbers. Keep each screen focused enough to read at a glance.

Choose a readable plot

  • Horizontal axis: select the supported axis and sweep range. The sweep plot’s left edge stays at 0°.
  • Curve appearance: white uses a thick solid bend trace, solid speed and dotted feather/square; color uses the same weights with solid colored lines.
  • Compare previous stroke: the previous trace is faint.
  • Show variability: mean curves can show ±1 standard deviation where at least two strokes support a sample.

Bend and oar speed use separate labeled axes with aligned zero lines. Bend scales to 1.3 times the previous peak, with a minimum upper limit of 0.5°. Live traces can be revised when the stroke finishes; completed boat/experimental curves remain visible through the next drive.

The variability band describes differences between strokes, not calibration accuracy or a confidence interval.

Two-oar and advanced metrics

With two configured oars and four connected sensors, choose either oar, Both or Average. Averages use matched strokes. Sculling totals combine the two physical oars for one rower; sweep summaries use the mean per rower with any combined measured total separately labeled.

Advanced metrics also contains experimental magnetic catch/finish angles and perpendicular position in drive. These need fresh magnetic samples, alignment and a supported heading reference; unavailable estimates show a dash.
