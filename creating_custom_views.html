IPHONE · ROWING VIEWS

Make the display your own.

Choose clear numbers and curves for the purpose of your row. Keep the explanations close with the app’s question-mark help.

Build and activate a view

  1. Open Rowing Views → Create & Edit Views.
  2. Choose a template such as Stroke Technique, Drive Focus or Metric Grid.
  3. Name the view, tap its metric slots and choose the values to show.
  4. For a plot view, choose the curves and check their units.
  5. Save it, then select it under Active Rowing Views. Arrange the active views in the order you want during the session.

Choose a metric grid for large numbers, a plot view for stroke shape, or a mixed layout for both. Available data depends on the connected sensors and recording options. Set up and activate your views before starting the row.

Choose the available measurements

Open Rowing Views → Available Metrics & Curves. Core choices start selected; Advanced/Experimental starts off. Measurements are grouped under Oar, Boat, GPS, Time and Body. Select the measurements you want available, then add them to the slots or plots in your rowing view.

Tap the ? beside an item to read what it measures and which inputs it needs. Changes to available choices apply to the next row or replay.

A dash means the required data are unavailable. Keep the phone fixed to the hull for boat motion, record GPS for ground speed, and connect a heart-rate source for heart rate.

Choose the curves you need

Start with the bend / moment / force curve and oar speed to follow loading through the drive. Add other oar or boat curves when they help answer your question. Check the question-mark explanation for the units, calibration and recording inputs.

Use supporting metric tiles for rate, piece time, distance or pace. Keep each screen focused enough to read at a glance.

Choose a readable plot

  • Horizontal axis: Time shows how the motion unfolds; Sweep angle shows measured travel from catch, starting at 0° with an adjustable range. Recovery follows the recorded motion back along that axis.
  • Displayed portion: Auto uses the drive portion when every selected curve is a drive curve. Drive portion ends at full feathering when that transition is available; Full stroke includes recovery.
  • Curve appearance: White or color styles distinguish the traces. Read the legend for the line style and use the labeled axes for units.
  • Compare previous stroke: adds a faint trace of the preceding stroke.
  • Show variability: adds a shaded ±1 standard deviation band to supported recorded curves where at least two strokes contribute.

Load and oar speed can share a plot with separate labeled axes. Bend scales to 1.3 times its supported peak, with a minimum upper limit of 0.5°. Missing inputs leave gaps.

Choose After drive separately for eligible load curves and drive measurements in Available Metrics & Curves. These can update when the drive finishes; yellow borders identify that choice. Power and other cycle-dependent measurements wait for the completed cycle.

Review recorded curves

Browse individual strokes and compare recorded selections after the row. Time views cover the selected stroke cycle plus a quarter-cycle on either side when neighboring samples are available. Vertical ranges stay consistent as you browse, and missing neighbors leave gaps.

Horizontal sweep can join smoothly to the next catch during the final half of recovery. This changes only the displayed join; the recorded motion, drive metrics, force and work stay unchanged.
