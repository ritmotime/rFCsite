Choose a few clear numbers and curves for the purpose of your row.

Build and activate a view

  1. Open Session Views → Create & Edit Views.
  2. Choose a template such as Live Technique, Drive Focus or a metric grid.
  3. Name the view, tap its metric slots and choose the values to show.
  4. For a plot view, choose the live curves and check their units.
  5. Save it, then select it under Active Workout Views. Arrange the views in the order you want during the session.

Choose a metric grid for large numbers, a plot view for stroke shape, or a mixed layout for both. Available data depends on the connected sensors and recording options.

Choose metrics by subject

Tap a metric slot, choose the detail level, then select a value under Oar, GPS, Time, Boat or Body. Core contains the everyday choices. Advanced adds more detail and includes every Core choice. New setups start at Advanced; your saved preference is retained.

SubjectUseful choices
OarPower, work per stroke, loaded stroke arc, catch miss, finish wash, peak moment and peak handle force.
GPSGround speed, pace, distance and course. Speed and pace are separate selections.
TimeStroke rate, drive time, piece time and workout time.
BoatSet range, fused boat speed, Speed check, Distance check and Energy-loss check. Advanced adds pitch, heave and turning measures.
BodyHeart rate and work-based calorie estimates.

A dash means the required data are unavailable. Keep the phone fixed to the hull for boat motion, record GPS for ground speed, and connect a heart-rate source for heart rate.

Choose curves for the question

CurveWhat it shows
Oar bendShaft loading and unloading, in degrees. A useful starting point with Oar speed.
Moment about pinBend converted to moment using the workout’s Moment/Bend Factor.
Estimated handle forceMoment divided by the saved inboard length. Select it separately from moment and bend.
Oar speedHow quickly the oar rotates, in degrees/second.
Feather / squareBlade rotation and its timing around the drive.
Horizontal sweep angleHorizontal oar travel from catch, averaged from the two aligned sensors. Available in Advanced; both sensors must have data.
Oar depth angle / Oar twistAdditional motion detail in Advanced. Enable their Include options in Workout Settings before starting. Depth angle is measured in degrees.
Boat motionFiltered acceleration, relative speed, relative distance and set. These need recorded phone motion.
Fused boat speedAbsolute ground speed combining GPS and phone motion.

Add rate, piece time, distance or GPS pace as supporting numbers. Keep each screen focused enough to read at a glance.

Choose a readable plot

  • Horizontal axis: Time shows drive and recovery; Sweep angle shows travel from catch. The sweep plot starts at 0°, with an adjustable maximum.
  • Curve appearance: White uses a thick load trace, solid speed and dotted feather/square. Color keeps the same line weights.
  • Compare previous stroke: adds a faint trace of the previous stroke.
  • Show variability: adds a shaded ±1 standard deviation band to mean curves where at least two strokes contribute.

The first selected bend, moment or handle-force curve can share a plot with oar speed on separate labeled axes. Bend scales to 1.3 times the previous peak, with a minimum upper limit of 0.5°. Boat curves update after each completed stroke and remain visible through the next drive.

The variability band shows differences between strokes. A missing input leaves a gap.

Compare two oars

With two configured oars and four connected sensors, choose either oar, Both or Average. Averages use matched strokes. Sculling totals combine the two physical oars for one rower; sweep summaries use the mean per rower with the combined measured total labeled separately.

Review recorded curves

Browse individual strokes or compare group averages after the row. Time views cover the selected stroke cycle plus a quarter-cycle on either side when neighboring samples are available. Vertical ranges stay consistent as you browse, and missing neighbors leave gaps.

Horizontal sweep can join smoothly to the next catch during the final half of recovery. This changes only the displayed join; the recorded motion, drive metrics, force and work stay unchanged.
