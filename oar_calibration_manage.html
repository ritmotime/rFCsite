Use this tab to organize, share, print labels for, import, export, select, and delete saved calibrations.

Common actions

  • Import CSV loads calibration records from a CSV file.
  • Export CSV creates a shareable CSV of saved calibrations.
  • Create Label PDF creates printable oar labels for selected calibrations.
  • Scan Label reads a QR label and adds or updates a saved calibration.
  • Saved calibrations can be filtered, deleted, or marked as the active calibration.

Printing labels

Label PDFs are useful when the same oar might be used with different phones, rowers, or boats. The labels include a QR code and short text such as calibration name, Cperm, SF, S1, S2, inboard, and outboard. After printing, place the label on or near the oar so the calibration can be restored by scanning it later.

Sharing calibrations

Use CSV export/import when you want to move several calibrations at once or share a calibration set with a coach or team. Use printed labels and QR scanning when you want a physical, oar-specific way to recover the setup.
