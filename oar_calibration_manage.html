Manage oars and collections

Organize oars, stiffness calibrations and alignments, and share a collection with your crew.

A separate collection of saved setups

Manage oars keeps named oars and collections. Measurements and edits made here stay in this collection; they do not change the active oar or Workout Settings. Choose and verify the active recording oar in Connect Oar Sensors.

  1. Use New oar or Copy from saved oars or calibrations.
  2. Check name, side, type, model and intended sensor pair.
  3. Choose or attach the saved alignment and stiffness calibration.
  4. Enter current dimensions and review the Moment/Bend Factor preview and its source.
  5. Create a New collection to group oars for a boat, crew or club.

Measure and maintain an oar

An oar’s detail screen offers alignment and Perform stiffness calibration. New measurements are saved to that managed oar. Use Edit details to select or attach other saved components.

Current dimensions may differ from the dimensions at the original calibration. Cperm from the same shaft can be reused to calculate a new MBF. The original component records keep their source and measurement history.

A manual MBF override takes priority; a manual Cperm override still needs dimensions. Review any sensor mismatch or missing-component message before using a setup.

Share or import a collection

  1. Open a collection and choose Share collection, or use Share all managed oars.
  2. Send the resulting JSON file using Mail, Messages or AirDrop.
  3. On the receiving phone, save it to Files.
  4. In Manage oars, choose Import managed oars, select the file, review it and tap Import copies.

Existing oars are kept. Imported oars remain in Manage oars and are not selected for a workout automatically.

Calibration CSV and printable QR labels belong to the corresponding saved-calibration/label tools where present in your build; they are distinct from the managed-collection JSON file. Scan an oar label from the connection workflow and verify the physical setup it describes.

Before using a shared setup

Check the exact shaft, sensor pair, positions, side, inboard/outboard, selected calibration and active override. A shared record cannot verify the current mounting. Repeat alignment after moving or replacing a sensor.
