iOS · RECORDING AND LIVE FEEDBACK

Row with the iOS beta.

Connect your saved oar, record the whole workout, and review the same session on your phone or in Cloud Analysis.

Get the iOS beta in TestFlight ↗Connect your sensors

These instructions describe the current iOS beta workflow. Update your installed beta for the latest recording and alignment steps. Recording on Android? Use the separate Android guide.

1. Choose and connect the oar

In Oar Sensors, use Scan oar label if the oar has a QR label. Otherwise select your saved oar or choose Setup new oar. Confirm the name, side, type and sensor pair, then connect both sensors. Enable Autoreconnect if you want the pair to reconnect next time.

Manage oars organizes a separate collection of saved oars and calibration components. Importing or editing that collection does not automatically change your active workout oar.

2. Align and check the scale

Open Oar Settings → Sensor Alignment. Complete the four core movements; the same series also offers optional Square swoop and Squared float. Save the alignment. Repeat it after moving or replacing a sensor.

Check the active Moment/Bend Factor (MBF, N·m/°). Use a measured stiffness calibration, a suitable existing Cperm with the current dimensions, or an explicitly chosen manual MBF estimate. Bend shape and timing come from the aligned sensors; calibrated moment, work and power need the correct scale.

Enter the oar dimensions and sensor positions even when using a manual factor. The recording retains those values for analysis.

3. Mount the phone and start the workout

Secure the iPhone to the boat so it moves with the hull. Keep it fixed throughout the session. Allow the required Bluetooth, motion and location permissions; phone motion and GPS provide the boat-motion and speed inputs.

Review Workout Settings and the Auto Piece Timer, choose a live view, then tap Start Session. Record through the work and recovery intervals. Stop the session when the whole workout is finished.

Workout settings → · Live views and curves →

4. Upload the whole workout

  1. Open Home → Cloud Analysis and sign in.
  2. Choose the saved workout and review its recorded oar and analysis settings.
  3. Tap Upload and Run Analysis. The app packages the available oar, phone, watch and GPS recordings with the saved setup.
  4. Open the completed report, download it, or choose Change Settings and Re-run.

For automatic upload, enable Upload after each workout. Upload begins after Stop Session or End Workout finishes saving. Pending uploads retry while the app is open and when you return.

After upload, iOS and Android use the same Cloud Analysis and report workflow. Re-running can reuse the existing server files while they remain available.

What the workout keeps

New recordings retain full-rate oar acceleration (AX/AY/AZ, including gravity) alongside the aligned gyro data, plus fresh magnetic packets when magnetic recording is enabled. Each oar’s dimensions, sensor positions, stiffness calibration, manual inputs and applied factor are captured with the workout, including when a manual factor is used.

The server shows the captured values under Recorded oar settings, separately from editable analysis settings. Changing the active oar later does not rewrite an earlier workout. Older recordings cannot gain acceleration or magnetic samples that were never saved.

Two oars on one phone

In Two-oar setup, enable Track two oars and assign two saved oars, with two sensors each. Tap Connect both oars. Each oar uses its own alignment, calibration and reference.

Choose either oar, Both or Average in the rowing view. For sculling, the individual readings describe each physical oar and rower work/power add both. For two sweep rowers, My oar and Teammate’s oar may be on the same or opposite sides; summaries show mean work/power per rower, with the measured total labeled separately. Heart rate belongs to My oar only.

Average curves use matched strokes; unmatched strokes remain available individually. A usable shared sync event can refine timing. Otherwise, timing uses sensor arrival and assumes equal Bluetooth delay.

Magnetic angle testing

Under experimental settings, open Magnetometer & catch angles. Record magnetometer & angle references is enabled by default at 2 requested polls/second, adjustable from 1–10. Check the fresh-packet diagnostics: a requested polling rate is not a guaranteed sensor sample rate.

Advanced metrics include Magnetic catch angle, Magnetic finish angle, Magnetic reference sweep arc and Perpendicular position in drive. Perpendicular is 0°; catch is normally negative and finish positive on either side. Estimates settle after the stroke and recovery, and missing references show a dash.

These are experimental iOS estimates requiring alignment and a supported boat-heading reference. Saving and uploading the magnetic data preserves it for later analysis; the latest server recording update does not introduce a new magnetic catch-angle calculation.
