RECORDING GUIDE

From the first connection to a saved workout.

Start here for your first row. If you’re away from the boat, try Replay first.

01Choose and connect the oar

Turn on the sensors and open Connect Oar. Choose a saved name, then tap Connect. Use Scan oar label if the oar has a QR label; check that its saved setup matches the physical oar and sensor positions.

For a new oar, choose Set up a new oar, connect its two sensors, enter the name, side and type, then tap Save and connect. Enable Connect automatically next time for the selected name if you want it remembered for the next app launch. Confirm that the expected sensors are connected before starting.

Manage Oars includes saved oars, named pairs and separate managed copies. Choosing a record to inspect does not change which oar is connected for rowing. Check whether you are editing the saved oar or an independent managed copy.

Full connection guide →

02Align the sensors and check the scale

Open Oar Settings → Align Sensors. Record Bias, Feather and square, Feathered level sweep and Square level sweep, then calculate and save the alignment. Repeat after moving, rotating or replacing a sensor.

After the alignment is saved, the optional Squared float captures a water reference. Square the blade, float it on the water with the boat level, hold steady for 12 seconds and tap Save to Oar Settings after an accepted capture. This reference does not change the alignment or bias.

Check the active Moment/Bend Factor (MBF, N·m/°). Use a measured stiffness calibration, a suitable existing Cperm with the current dimensions, or an explicitly chosen manual MBF estimate. Bend shape and timing come from the aligned sensors; calibrated moment, work and power need the correct scale.

Saving a new measured stiffness calibration selects its Cperm and clears manual MBF and manual Cperm overrides. Enter the oar dimensions and sensor positions even when using a manual factor; the recording retains them for analysis.

Guided sensor alignment → · Stiffness, dimensions and overrides →

03Mount the phone and record the workout

Secure the iPhone to the boat so it moves with the hull. Keep it fixed throughout the session. Allow Bluetooth, motion and location access when requested; phone motion and GPS provide the boat-motion and speed inputs.

Review Workout Settings and the Auto Piece Timer. In Rowing Views, choose the views you want to use, then tap Start Rowing on Home. Record through the work and recovery intervals. Tap End Row when the whole workout is finished.

The app saves the recording and finishes its analysis. Home and workout history show the progress; history may display Finishing analysis — you can start another row while the workout is being prepared. Wait for it to finish before opening that workout’s complete results. Return to the app if processing or a pending upload needs to resume.

In Rowing Views → Available Metrics & Curves, Core choices start selected. Add your selected numbers and curves to a view under Create & Edit Views, then activate it in Active Rowing Views. A missing input leaves the dependent measurement unavailable.

Workout settings → · Create a rowing view →

04Upload the workout
  1. On Home, open ritmoSwingLab and sign in. Tap Analyze latest or Choose a workout.
  2. Under Processing → Method, choose Server · raw recording to recalculate the row. To build a report from calculations already saved on the phone, choose Onboard · saved results (experimental).
  3. Review the oar setup and report choices, then tap the upload button for your method. Open the report when processing finishes.

Enable Upload after each workout for automatic uploads. Return to the app to resume a pending upload.

Processing methods and re-analysis

05Review the saved workout

Open Workout History and choose the row. A piece is a continuous work interval. A group collects similar strokes, even when they occurred at different times. To change either, open Adjust Workout Settings, choose APT Settings or Stroke Group Settings, then tap Apply.

Recorded curve views let you browse individual strokes and compare selections. Time views include a quarter-cycle before and after the selected cycle when neighboring data exist. Vertical ranges stay consistent while browsing, and supported curves can show a variability band when enough strokes contribute.

The cloud report adds the session overview, map, animation and report-view presets. Live values can change after a stroke finishes or the workout is processed with its complete recording.

Use the completed report →

06Understand what the recording keeps

The workout saves the available oar motion, phone motion, GPS and heart-rate data, together with the oar dimensions, sensor positions, alignment, stiffness calibration and applied factor. Enter the dimensions even when using a manual factor.

In ritmoFCTest, Files → Raw recording storage starts at Keep until I delete. Choose 7 days, 30 days or a custom duration for future recordings. Saved results stay available.

The upload screen shows this snapshot under Recorded oar settings. Changing the active oar later leaves an earlier workout’s setup intact. Reprocessing older workouts requires their original recordings; a saved summary cannot supply missing sensor data.

Try a recorded row without sensors

On Home, open Advanced/Diagnostics → Replay and turn on Replay recorded data. Tap Workout, choose Built-in or a saved workout, then set the replay speed.

Return Home and tap Start Replay to try your rowing views. Turn off Replay recorded data before your next live row.
